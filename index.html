Old Priory Judo Club

Judo in York since 1947.

Old Priory is a long-established York judo club with a welcoming approach to beginners and experienced judoka alike.

Our focus is good judo, good values and an environment where young people can enjoy learning at their own pace. The club is associated with Bushido International / Za-Zen.

New starters

Your first session should feel easy.

No experience? No problem. The free trial is there so your child can see whether judo is right for them without pressure.

Do we need a judo suit?

No special equipment is needed for a first visit. Comfortable sports clothing is fine unless the club advises otherwise.

What age can children start?

The club welcomes boys and girls from age 5 upwards.

Does my child need experience?

Not at all. Beginners are welcome and sessions are suitable for a range of abilities.

What happens after the trial?

If they enjoy it, the club can explain membership, kit, grading and which regular session suits them best.

Where we train

Our dojo.

A bright, dedicated training space with a full-size matted area for safe, structured judo practice.
