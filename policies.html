Cookies and website technologies

Old Priory Judo Club keeps its use of cookies and similar technologies to a minimum. We do not currently use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, tracking pixels or browser storage for marketing or visitor profiling.

Essential member session cookie

When you sign in to the members area, the website sets a first-party cookie named opjc_session. It is used only to keep you securely signed in and to associate your browser with your authenticated session. The cookie is marked Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, and is configured to expire after a maximum of 14 days. Signing out invalidates the session and removes the cookie from the browser.

This cookie is necessary to provide the members service you have requested. It is not used for analytics, advertising or tracking across other websites.

Google Maps

The Contact page does not load Google Maps automatically. A connection to Google is made only if you choose to load the map. Once loaded, Google may use cookies or similar technologies under its own terms and privacy arrangements.

Other third-party services

Links to external services such as Facebook do not load those services into the page. They are contacted only when you choose to follow the link. If we introduce non-essential cookies, analytics or other embedded services in future, we will update this notice and obtain consent where required.

Accessibility

We want the Old Priory Judo Club website to be usable by as many people as reasonably possible. The site is designed with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA in mind.

In particular, we aim to provide clear page structure and headings, keyboard-accessible navigation and controls, labelled form fields, meaningful alternative text for informative images, readable colour contrast, responsive layouts, and content that remains usable when text is enlarged or the page is zoomed.

Current status

The site is still being developed and has not yet undergone an independent accessibility audit, so we do not currently claim formal WCAG 2.2 AA conformance. Third-party content that you choose to load, such as Google Maps, may also have accessibility characteristics outside our direct control.

Reporting an accessibility problem

If you find something difficult to use or need information in a different format, please contact the club and tell us which page or feature is causing the problem. We will use that feedback to improve the site where we reasonably can.
