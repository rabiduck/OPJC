About Shotokan

Karate with strong foundations.

Shotokan is a major style of Japanese Karate associated with Gichin Funakoshi and the development of Karate on mainland Japan during the twentieth century.

Training combines basic techniques (kihon), formal patterns (kata) and partner work (kumite). The emphasis is on learning sound technique, control, discipline and continual improvement.
